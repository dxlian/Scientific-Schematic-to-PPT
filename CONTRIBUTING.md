# Contributing

## Share a figure / 分享案例

Use the [community submission form](https://github.com/dxlian/Scientific-Schematic-to-PPT/issues/new?template=share-your-figure.yml) to share a preview, editable-file link, source references, and your preferred attribution. Read the [English guide](docs/community-gallery.en.md) or [中文说明](docs/community-gallery.md). Submissions remain in Issues until reviewed; no uploaded code or macros are run automatically. Community contributions retain the license declared by their authors.

## Improve the skill

Keep changes focused on the evidence-to-design-to-review-to-native-PPT workflow. Do not add automatic public uploads, unapproved paid services, proprietary font redistribution, or approval bypasses.

Contributions should preserve the distinction between restrained styling and complete scientific content. Include a clear explanation of the change and, for drawing improvements, an original example showing the affected behavior. Keep published examples free of personal information and use materials you are entitled to share.

Use synthetic diagrams or materials you have permission to share. Report limitations honestly. Do not infer scientific correctness from a PPTX structural pass.
