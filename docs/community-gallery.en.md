# Community Gallery

[中文](community-gallery.md) · [Home](../README.en.md) · [Built-in examples](gallery.en.md)

I would like this gallery to grow beyond the built-in examples and reflect how researchers use the skill across disciplines. Share a schematic you have made, along with any useful design choices.

**[Submit your case](https://github.com/dxlian/Scientific-Schematic-to-PPT/issues/new?template=share-your-figure.yml)**

## How to submit

1. Open the submission form and describe the figure's title, discipline, and scientific purpose.
2. Attach a clear preview to the Issue and provide a public download link for the editable PPT. Scene JSON is optional. Do not upload macros, executables, or access tokens.
3. Note the skill version, key instructions, manual changes, and which elements are native objects or retained images.
4. Specify your preferred credit, source references, and license, and confirm that the material can be shared publicly.

Do not upload confidential research, personal information, or material with unclear sharing rights. Full papers, experimental datasets, and private conversation records are not required.

## Selection

I will review the submission details, source information, sharing permissions, preview, and stated editing scope. Selected work will appear here with links to the original submission, author, and files. Requests for clarification or revisions will stay in the Issue.

Submissions are not automatically merged, and uploaded code or macros are never run automatically. Authors remain responsible for scientific accuracy; inclusion is not an independent validation of a paper's conclusions.

## Featured work

Submissions are open. Each selected entry will include a preview, discipline, author credit, editable-file link, original Issue, and license.

## Credit and licensing

Authors retain ownership and attribution. The submission permission covers displaying the preview and description in this project and linking to the supplied files. Redistribution or reuse of the complete files follows the author's stated license. Work without clear permission will not be featured, and community contributions do not automatically become MIT-licensed.

Request attribution changes, link updates, or removal from the gallery in the original Issue.
